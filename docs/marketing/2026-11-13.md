## 본문 초안
I've been poking at a browser Battleship engine and wanted to see how much placement actually matters, so I had its probability-based ("Hard") CPU play against different fleet layouts (2,000 each):

- Random fleet: 46.3 shots on average
- Everything on the outer rim: 53.8
- Everything in the inner 6x6: 44.2
- Ships touching vs. not touching: no real difference (46.5 vs 45.3)

Also: a random fleet covers a central square 20.7% of the time but a corner only 7.5%, which is exactly where a density-based hunter starts. Caveat: this is one engine's AI — a human who learns you hug the edge will adapt.
